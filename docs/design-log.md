## Wireframe review plan

After Week 1, two classmates walk through five tasks:

1. Find a room with 4+ seats and a whiteboard, and check in.
2. Create a course group and join it from a second account.
3. Propose a session that conflicts, then revise it.
4. Start a shared focus timer, go offline, and reconnect.
5. Enter five tasks and generate a study plan.

Record what confused them and what changed here.
